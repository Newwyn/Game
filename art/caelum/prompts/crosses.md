File name `caelum-crosses`. A 2D game asset sheet: landscape canvas 3:2, an invisible grid of 3 columns x 2 rows = 6 equal cells. In each cell ONE upright Latin cross standing on its foot, drawn front-on, perfectly straight and symmetrical, crisp clean line art, soft cel shading, centred, filling about 80%% of the cell height, nothing touching the cell borders:
cell 1 = LIFE cross: white gold frame with emerald-green stained glass and a green gem;
cell 2 = MIGHT cross: white gold frame with crimson-red stained glass and a red gem;
cell 3 = THUNDER cross: dark silver frame with electric violet-blue stained glass and a crackling violet gem;
cell 4 = PURITY cross: white silver frame with pale sky-blue stained glass and a clear white gem;
cell 5 = the GIANT HOLY cross: much more ornate, radiant white gold with golden stained glass and a blazing white-gold gem, small wings of light at the crossing;

No characters, no ground, no glow halo around the crosses, no text. Background: perfectly flat pure MAGENTA #FF00FF everywhere (it will be removed), no gradient.
