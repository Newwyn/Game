## Build
1. **Class `crosspriest`** in `CLASS_DEFS`: name `Caelum`, title `Thánh Giá` (or similar Vietnamese), race `elf`, `classGroup: 'priest'`, `dmgType: 'magic'`, ranged. Fragile support-caster stats in the range of Seraphine / Elarion (balance later). Moves by floating (same movement, no special rule). Shop price 1200.
2. **Crosses (skill 1)** as server-side field objects owned by the caster (like YunYou's formation): `{ id, type 1..4, x, z, level 1..3, placedAt, levelAt, expireAt, ownerSide }`. Exactly as in the spec: 4 types and their ally / enemy effects, pulse rates (lightning every 1 s; others per the spec's proposals), level up every 10 s, level 3 lasts 10 s, radius grows with level (3 / 3.6 / 4.2), max 4 crosses per caster, the 5th replaces the OLDEST and inherits its level and running timer (if the replaced one was level 3, the new one gets a fresh 10 s), stacking rule (only lightning damage and healing stack across overlapping crosses of the same type; other effects take the highest level), lightning stun 0.5 s with a 2 s per-target immunity, crosses cannot be targeted or damaged. Cost 2 Orb, cooldown 5 s.
   - **Type selection**: the player picks the type (1..4) before casting, like Elarion's picker; store `member.crossType` on the server (default 1); socket event to change it; AUTO picks the type itself (spec §4 AUTO rule).
   - **Placement**: the cast carries a target point `{ x, z }` from the client. The server clamps it to the cast range (14 units from Caelum) and to the battlefield bounds, then places the cross. If no point is sent (tap without drag, or AUTO), the server chooses the best point by the spec's AUTO rule (cover the most units of the side the type affects; avoid the exact spot of an existing cross of the same type).
3. **Skill 2** (2 Orb, cooldown 7 s, shrinking with skill level like other heroes' cooldown scaling): every cross of the caster immediately triggers its effect 3 times; crosses below level 3 get 4 s taken off their time to next level, level-3 crosses get +4 s of remaining life.
4. **Skill 3** (4 Orb, cooldown 20 s): a giant holy cross at the CENTRE of the battlefield, whole-field effect, lasts 10 s, Holy Smite every 1 s on all enemies (damage + debuffs: damage dealt down, defence down, strip one buff per hit), allies get % attack and % defence for its duration, every 5th smite also explodes on all enemies and removes all debuffs from allies. Not counted in the 4-cross limit. Cancelled if Caelum dies.
5. **Talents**: talent 1 — each cross of the caster on the field gives +4% attack to the whole team (max 4 → +16%). Talent 2 — each of the caster's skill-1 crosses that ends (replaced or expired) refunds 1 Orb; when Caelum dies, all his remaining crosses fly to him and revive him with 20% max HP per cross (max 80%), those crosses are consumed; no cross = no revive; 30 s cooldown on the revive. Make sure PvP always ends (revive has a cooldown and needs crosses; Sinh Tử damage after 3:00 still applies).
6. **Drag-and-drop input (client, reusable)**: a small generic module (e.g. `src/ui/dragCast.js`) any hero can opt into via a flag on the skill (`dragTarget: { range, radius(level) }`). On the skill button: pointer down + move beyond a small threshold starts a drag; while dragging, show on the ground a CLEAR, easy-to-read area ring in the colour of the selected cross type plus a translucent ghost cross following the pointer (raycast to the ground plane); outside range the ring turns red and snaps to the range edge; release over the field = cast with `{ x, z }`; release back over the button = cancel (no Orb spent); a quick tap without drag = cast without a point (server auto-places). Works with mouse and touch (pointer events), does not pause the battle, does not break the existing click-to-cast of every other hero. The AUTO toggle keeps working.
7. **Type picker UI (client)**: on Caelum's card, 4 small buttons above / next to skill 1 to choose the cross type (colour + short Vietnamese name: Sinh Mệnh, Uy Lực, Sấm Sét, Thanh Tẩy), the selected one highlighted, same feel as Elarion's tray.
8. **Visuals (temporary until the drawn art exists — keep it clean and replaceable)**: use Seraphine's sprite (`priest`) as a placeholder for Caelum. Crosses: a simple placeholder billboard per type (coloured quad) — the lead will supply drawn images later at `src/assets/sprites/caelum_cross_1..4.webp` and `caelum_cross_big.webp`; load them with `import.meta.glob` so the game works whether they exist or not. Placed-cross area: a SOFT, subtle ring (faint, a few light motes) — must not cover the battle. Level shown by size / brightness. Effects: prefer the vendored pixelfx library (`src/vfx/pixelFx.js` `LIB`: lightning, heal, shield, holy hit, explosion…), normal pixel scale (never scaled into big blocks). New `src/vfx/crossFx.js` + events handler `crossFx` in `src/combat/events.js`. Status icons / names in `src/ui/hud.js`; skill and talent descriptions in `src/ui/heroPanel.js` (Vietnamese, full diacritics). Reuse existing icons as placeholders.
9. **Tests** — new `tests/crosspriest.test.js` with the vm harness of `tests/swordsage.test.js`: placement with a point (clamped to range and field), auto placement without a point, each type's ally and enemy effects inside vs outside the radius, pulse timing, level-up timing and radius growth, level-3 10 s life, 5th cross replaces the oldest and inherits level / timer (and fresh 10 s case), stacking rule, lightning stun + 2 s immunity, crosses untargetable, skill 2 triple trigger and timer changes, skill 3 timeline (10 smites, 2 explosions, ally buffs, debuff removal, centre position), talent 1 stacking, talent 2 Orb refund and revive (HP per cross, crosses consumed, 30 s cooldown, no crosses = no revive), AUTO uses all three skills and picks types, a long AUTO battle and a PvP mirror both end with a winner.
