Bo che do Do hoa Pixel: Ignar + Sylva ve lai tranh ve tay (giu tranh goc pixel trong art/)

Tran dau de nhin hon: an bang chi so o che do Dau, so sat thuong xep cot, mau phe xanh/do, giam choi hieu ung

- Che do Dau: bang chi so an mac dinh (phim I hien lai, game nho lua chon rieng Test / Dau)
- So sat thuong: xep cot theo nguoi trung don, chi mang to + nay, so phu (BLOCK, khien, MISS) nho va mo
- Phan biet phe: dia sang duoi chan xanh (minh) / do (dich), thanh mau dich mau do, vien theo mau phe
- Giam choi: nhieu hieu ung no cung luc thi hieu ung cong sang tu mo bot (toi da 40%)

Do hoa Pixel: nut chon trong bang Tam dung + 1 ban ro nhat + hieu ung Ignar cung co o (cau lua roi ve lai)

Thu che do Pixel (phim X) + Ignar pixel art day du + hieu ung pixel Ignar/Sylva

- Do hoa Pixel: chon trong bang Tam dung (nut Do hoa), chi giu ban ro nhat
- Ignar pixel: cau lua roi thang xuong; Ho Ve Than Lua co dong tac chuong + gam

- 21 hieu ung lua pixel cho Ignar + 10 hieu ung pixel cho Sylva (thu vien pixelfx, MIT)

Sylva hoan thien: hieu ung ve tay (9 tam) + Gau ui hien dung + phanh quay dau + Dai Bang con rieng
